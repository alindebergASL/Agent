# University of Iowa discovery brief

*Reference answer for a fictional 30-minute meeting · November 27, 2026*

**Decision to take into the room.** Proceed with a small staff FAQ pilot in the university ChatGPT Edu workspace using public service pages. Ask the research support lead to own the accuracy of every access instruction. This gives the team a useful draft without needing any research records. [Meeting input](meeting-scope.txt#L4) · [Named reviewer](meeting-scope.txt#L5) · [ITS guidance on ChatGPT Edu](https://its.uiowa.edu/services/chatgpt-edu/how-use-chatgpt-edu)

**A concrete first question for the FAQ.** “Where do I start if my research requires UI Hospitals and Clinics clinical data?” For an EHR extract, Iowa’s Biomedical Informatics Core lists the EHR and EDW4R clinical data request. A researcher who needs an IHDR Data Enclave account has a separate route through the enclave support team. Have the lead open both links and check that the FAQ sends staff to the right place. [ICTS data requests](https://icts.uiowa.edu/research-service-cores/biomedical-informatics-core/data-access-and-tools) · [IHDR Data Enclave](https://research.its.uiowa.edu/our-services/storage-services/iowa-health-data-resource-ihdr-data-enclave)

**Use the meeting to decide three things.**

- Which two questions do staff answer most often, and which public page is authoritative for each answer?
- Can the research support lead check each draft against its source and flag steps that require an individual decision?
- Would one week of comparing drafts with staff-written answers show whether the FAQ saves time and stays accurate?

**First deliverable.** One reviewed answer with its source link and reviewer recorded. This carries forward the earlier brief’s recommendation to start with a low-risk task and name the person who checks it.
